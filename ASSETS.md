# Third-party assets

Robot meshes and textures are bundled under
`src/tb4_voice_navigation/models/`. Their sources and checksums are recorded in
`asset_manifest.json` in that directory.

| Asset | Source | License notice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unitree G1 and Go2 | [unitree_ros](https://github.com/unitreerobotics/unitree_ros/tree/725b59b6de39a26c425632120c89f23c49a36f1e/robots), commit `725b59b6de39a26c425632120c89f23c49a36f1e` | BSD-3-Clause; each model includes `LICENSE` |
| UR5e and UR3e | [Universal Robots ROS 2 Description](https://github.com/UniversalRobots/Universal_Robots_ROS2_Description), Jazzy 3.5.1 | See each model's `COPYRIGHT` |
| X500 | [PX4 Gazebo Models](https://github.com/PX4/PX4-gazebo-models/tree/main/models/x500_base) | BSD-3-Clause; `x500_base/LICENSE` |
| TurtleBot 4 exhibit | [Nav2 minimal TurtleBot simulation](https://github.com/ros-navigation/nav2_minimal_turtlebot_simulation) | `turtlebot4_display/COPYRIGHT` |

Exhibit models use static visual poses. G1 and Go2 poses are assembled from their
URDF joint transforms. Exhibit motor, sensor, and TF plugins are omitted; X500
materials are adapted for Gazebo Harmonic. Original license notices remain with
the assets. The moving TurtleBot uses the separate Nav2 simulation package.

PAIRS Lab and VNU logos were supplied for this educational scene. They remain
separate from the software license; no ownership of the logos is claimed.
Architecture, furniture, signs, and test areas are generated by this project's
scene scripts.
